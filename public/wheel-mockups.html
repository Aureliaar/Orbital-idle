Wheel Mockups

Six treatments for showing competing yield upgrades on a circle of fifths

A · Bare Nodes

What we just shipped. Discrete circles with level badges, dashed flow arrows between them. Clean, but each node is an island.

+ simple, readable
+ flow arrows show cost direction
- nodes don't show what they cost
- no sense of the circle as a flow network

B · Arc Segments

Each note owns a wedge of a thick ring. The arc fills clockwise as you save toward the next level. Invested notes are saturated; untouched ones are ghosted.

+ progress is spatial — you see the ring "filling up"
+ the whole wheel reads at a glance as a pie chart of investment
- no cost-flow arrows — the spending relationship is implicit
- harder to tap individual segments on mobile

C · Growth Rings

Each level adds a concentric ring, like growth rings on a tree. High-level nodes swell outward; untouched ones stay small. The visual weight directly encodes investment.

+ level is size — no numbers needed, instant read
+ satisfying growth: buying a level visibly enlarges the node
+ high-invest nodes naturally dominate the composition
- can crowd neighbours at high levels
- progress toward next level needs a partial-ring or other cue

D · River Flow

Arrows between nodes vary in thickness: fat = lots of that currency has been spent upgrading the downstream node. The wheel becomes a Sankey-style flow diagram. You see at a glance where you've been pumping resources.

+ the competition is visceral — thick pipes starve thin ones
+ history is visible: you see past choices, not just current state
- needs careful stroke-width scaling to stay legible
- the "cost" relationship (C costs G) is backwards from the "flow" direction — potentially confusing

E · Gauge Dials

Each node is a miniature gauge dial: filled arcs show completed levels, a fainter partial arc shows progress. The arc is the upgrade itself — tap to "wind it forward." Clean, instrument-like.

+ each node encodes two things: level and progress
+ instrument aesthetic matches the observatory skin
+ compact — more room for cost tooltips or detail on tap
- arcs at small radius are hard to read precisely
- no direct encoding of the cost relationship

F · Constellation

A graph where edges are harmonic intervals. Upgrading a note brightens every edge it touches. The decision becomes "which subgraph do I light up?" Directly encodes the music-theory resonance structure — P5 lines, M3 lines, etc.

+ serves the isomorphism — the visual is the harmonic web
+ decisions feel spatial: "strengthen the C-G-D backbone or fan out to E?"
+ star-chart aesthetic fits the observatory skin perfectly
- dense at 7 nodes × 21 possible edges
- the cost direction (circle of fifths) is lost in the all-pairs view
- needs interaction (tap to buy) layered on top
