Yield Ring on Resonator

The yield wheel lives as an outer ring on the helix itself — same chroma angles,
one "octave" beyond the partial cloud. Mostly dormant; lights up once all 7 notes unlock.

A · Dormant Embers

Before the yield panel unlocks: tiny ember dots sit at each note's chroma angle on a faint outer guide ring. You might not even notice them. Once all 7 notes unlock, they wake up.

+ zero clutter in the early game
+ the "oh, those dots were always there" reveal
+ positions already encode the circle-of-fifths topology

B · Awakened Gauges

Once all 7 unlock, the embers grow into gauge nodes. Invested notes swell; gauge arcs show level + progress. Cost-flow arcs trace the outer ring between nodes. Leveled nodes are tappable to buy the next level. Uninvested nodes stay small and faint.

+ the yield ring IS the helix's outermost octave
+ investment reads as size — big nodes dominate the rim
+ gauge arcs encode level + progress in one shape
- nodes at the edge may clip on small screens

C · Bloom Petals

Each yield level adds a "petal" — an elliptical glow radiating outward from the helix rim. Lvl 0 = a dim dot. Lvl 3 = three nested petals flaring out like a solar prominence. The helix grows visual weight where you invest. Very organic, very observatory.

+ beautiful — the helix literally blooms where you invest
+ no hard edges, blends with the cloud aesthetic
+ zero early-game clutter — just dots, then petals emerge
- hard to read precise level without counting petals
- no affordance for "tap to buy" — needs a separate interaction

D · Compass Extension

No ring at all — instead, each compass spoke extends outward by an amount proportional to its yield level, with tick marks for each completed level. Like a clock hand or compass needle that grows longer as you invest. The helix becomes a star with uneven rays.

+ very clean — no new shapes, just longer lines
+ investment asymmetry is instantly readable (long vs short rays)
+ tick marks give precise level at a glance
+ dormant = normal compass, awakened = compass grows
- long spokes cross the coincidence zone — visual conflict with partials
- less obvious where to tap to buy

E · Arc Wedge Ring

A thin ring hugging the helix's outer edge, divided into 7 wedges. Wedge fill opacity = yield level. Dormant = the ring is barely there. Invested = the relevant wedges glow. The simplest integration — adds one visual layer without any new shapes.

+ minimal — one ring, no nodes, no arrows
+ the "mostly hidden" state is just an almost-invisible ring
+ tapping a wedge to buy feels natural
+ reads as a progress/EQ meter around the instrument
- hard to show multiplier or level number
- cost-flow relationship is invisible
- less "wow" factor than nodes or petals

F · Hybrid: Ring + Expandable Nodes

Default: a whisper-quiet ring with tiny dots encoding level (like pips on a watch dial). Tapping a dot — or any dot becoming affordable — expands it into a detail card showing level, multiplier, cost, and progress. Only one card open at a time. The cost arrow reveals itself only for the expanded node.

+ best of both: quiet ring in idle, full detail on demand
+ cost-flow arrow only appears in context — not always cluttering
+ the "card" can show exact cost, progress bar, multiplier transition
+ affordable nodes could auto-pulse to draw attention
- the expanded card partially overlaps the helix (but partials fly past it anyway)
- interaction model needs clear "tap to expand / tap to buy" distinction
